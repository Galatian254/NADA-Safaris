About Nada Afrika

Mindfulness, harmony, and the union of adventure with the soul

Journey Within as You Explore Africa

Our name "Nada" draws inspiration from the yoga philosophy of "Nada yoga" which explores the profound connection between sound and consciousness. We offer a unique way to experience Africa, inviting you to journey within as you explore the wonders of the continent.

Our vision is to lead travelers on a transformative odyssey where they find alignment, serenity and oneness in the African landscape. We aspire to awaken a deep sense of connection and consciousness in every traveler, fostering a profound appreciation for the beauty and diversity of Africa.

Our mission is to create a sanctuary for travelers; where they can reconnect with nature, culture and their inner selves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our safari services

How do I know if my payment was successful?

After payment, you'll receive a confirmation with a booking ID. You can also check your booking status in the "My Bookings" section.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa only. All transactions are secure and processed through verified channels.

Can I cancel or modify my booking?

Yes, you can cancel or modify your booking up to 48hours after payment. Please contact us for assistance with changes.

Do you provide travel insurance?

We recommend purchasing travel insurance for all trips. We can help you arrange suitable coverage through our partners.

What should I bring on my safari?

Bring valid ID, appropriate clothing for the destination, medications, and any required documents. We'll provide a detailed packing list upon booking.

Are your tours guided?

Most of our tours include professional guides. Private tours can be arranged with or without guides based on your preference.
